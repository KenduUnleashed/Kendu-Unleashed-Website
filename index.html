Our story

Backed by the Kendu Community, we’re here to support the next generation of skaters, riders, racers, and shredders.

Born from action sports, Kendu Unleashed represents those who refuse to settle. Our mission is simple: support athletes, empower communities, and inspire progression at every level.

We’re here for the athletes grinding in the park or shredding the waves, the kids chasing their first podium and the fans who live for the energy of competition. Whether it’s through apparel, events, or athlete sponsorships, Kendu Unleashed exists to support the journey of all athletes and celebrate the moments that matter.

Sponsorships

Support that grows with you.

At Kendu Unleashed, athletes aren’t just supported — they’re family. Our sponsorships are designed to grow with you, from first contests to major podiums.

We support athletes who embody the Kendu mindset — disciplined, creative, and community-driven. Our sponsorships help riders, racers, and skaters unlock their potential while giving them a platform to inspire others.

What we look for

Passion, discipline, creativity, and a community-driven mindset.

  • Dedication in your sport and beyond
  • Progression, creativity, and resilience
  • A willingness to represent the Kendu movement with pride
Explore sponsorship levels

Bronze – Flow Support

For up-and-coming athletes proving themselves in the scene.

  • Starter Gear Pack (1 t-shirt + 1 hat + 1 sticker pack)
  • Shoutouts & reposts on Kendu social media
  • Name listed on the Kendu Community page
  • Access to exclusive Kendu community events

Silver – Community Athlete

For athletes actively competing and representing in their local scene.

  • All Bronze benefits
  • Quarterly Merch Allowance (choice of 2–3 apparel items per season)
  • Social media collabs + feature post on Kendu channels
  • Discounts on additional Kendu gear
  • Priority consideration for Gold level

Gold – Sponsored Athlete

For established athletes making podiums and leading their community.

  • All Silver benefits
  • Full Merch Support (event gear, hoodie, multiple tees, hat, and accessories)
  • Dedicated highlight post + athlete feature on Kendu channels
  • Partial event registration support (case by case)
  • Early access to new product drops & prototype gear
  • Athlete profile spotlighted on Kendu site
